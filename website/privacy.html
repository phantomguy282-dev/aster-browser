Privacy & transparency

Your data deserves
a clear explanation.

Policy for this preview distribution. This is a technical disclosure, not a claim that the browser makes you anonymous.

The website

This static website includes no analytics, advertising scripts, remote fonts or tracking cookies. It requests its own pages, images, release metadata and installer files. A future hosting provider may log IP addresses, request times and user agents. Its identity and retention policy must be disclosed by the publisher before public deployment.

Local browser data

Aster keeps bookmarks, history, settings, themes and normal session information in your local profile. Chromium stores cookies, cache and website storage. Downloaded files remain where you save them. Configuration exports may contain bookmarks and preferences; treat them as personal data.

Private sessions

Private tabs use a separate temporary browser session and are not added to normal browsing history. Explicitly saved bookmarks and downloaded files remain. Private mode does not hide your activity from websites, a VPN provider, your employer or internet provider.

Accounts and migration

Configured Google account connections use Google's official OAuth endpoints and your system browser. Aster does not collect Google passwords. Account tokens are stored using operating-system encryption; only minimal identity scopes are requested. Google website cookies and Chrome Sync are not supplied by this connection.

Browser import is initiated by you, with a selected source and data categories. It does not bypass OS encryption or extract protected passwords, autofill secrets or cookies. Close the source browser as instructed to avoid inconsistent history snapshots.

Protection and optional services

Downloading filter lists contacts their listed HTTPS providers. Their rules are evaluated locally. Opening websites still sends normal network requests to those websites and their permitted services. Using a configured VPN involves Windows and your chosen provider; routing, DNS and retention policies belong to that configuration.

Update checks, when configured, contact the publisher's HTTPS endpoint. Updates require signed metadata and integrity verification. No production update endpoint or trust infrastructure is implied by this website.

Extensions and control

Extensions can read and modify data allowed by their permissions. Only install trusted extension code. Use the privacy dashboard to review protection, cookies, permissions and browsing-data controls; use account disconnect to remove the local connection and attempt remote token revocation.

Operator and privacy contact

No public operator identity or privacy contact has been supplied for this preview. These fields are a deployment requirement, not an invitation to send personal data to an invented address. Contact the publisher through the channel where you obtained the build.
